Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Our Commitment to Privacy

At ZORUVI, we believe in a lean, respectful, and privacy-first web architecture. We strictly avoid invasive tracking cookies, third-party advertising scripts, and unnecessary data collection. Your data belongs to you and your organization.

2. Information We Collect

To provide our operational performance and career management service, we collect only the essential information required to operate your account:

• Account Details: Email address, unique username, and country of operation provided during registration.
• Operational Metrics: Performance data (such as AHT, QA scores, adherence, and SNPS) securely stored and isolated via database-level Row Level Security (RLS).

3. Cookies and Analytics

We do not use commercial tracking cookies, user profiling beacons, or intrusive marketing trackers. For high-level, anonymous traffic insights, we utilize GoatCounter—an open-source, privacy-focused analytics tool that does not store personal data, track users across websites, or use cookies.

4. How We Use Your Information

The collected data is used exclusively to:
• Authenticate your user session and maintain security.
• Provide real-time visualization of your operational performance metrics.
• Manage workspace permissions and squad governance.

5. Data Security & Storage

Your data is hosted securely using industry-standard infrastructure (Supabase). We employ robust data isolation practices, ensuring that your operational metrics remain accessible solely to you and authorized members within your enterprise hierarchy.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You maintain full control over your personal information. You have the right to access, correct, or request complete deletion of your account and associated profile data at any time by contacting our support channels.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, you can reach out through our official platform channels.
